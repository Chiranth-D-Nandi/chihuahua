Metrics Glossary & Interpretation

The Injection Success Rate (ISR) measures the percentage of adversarial inputs that successfully forced the Vision-Language Model (VLM) to execute the attacker's target tool call. A high ISR indicates a severe vulnerability where the model failed to resist the prompt injection. The Structural Similarity (SSIM) index measures the visual degradation between the clean and poisoned images; a score close to 1.0 means the poisoned image is indistinguishable from the original to the human eye, making the attack highly stealthy. The Semantic Drift Score (SDS) calculates the cosine distance between the text embeddings of the model's clean response versus its poisoned response. A high drift score means the model's behavior was successfully hijacked, shifting away from its intended task.

To prove that the VLM is reacting to the mathematical perturbations and not just randomly hallucinating, we measure the Caption-Label Pearson r (CLPR), which tracks the correlation between the perturbation budget (epsilon) and the ISR. Furthermore, the B2 Attention Monitor tracks the Attention Deviation Rate (ADR) and Attention Entropy Rate (AER) to detect abnormal, sparse transformer attention distributions that occur during a successful attack. Finally, in multi-agent environments, the Context Poisoning Rate (CPR) and Context Buffer Depth (CBD) track how many hops a malicious worm was able to propagate through shared agent memory before being stopped by the defenses.

Benchmark Parameters

The evaluations were conducted with strict baseline parameters. The R0 Backdoor trigger was applied with an alpha opacity of 1.0 (a fully opaque trigger). The R1 Pixel Sweep evaluated attacks across escalating epsilon budgets of 1/255, 2/255, and 4/255 to measure the attack's robustness, while the Live Pipeline defaults to an aggressive epsilon of 8/255 for FGSM and PGD attacks. The R2 Attention Poisoning utilized 100 PGD optimization steps targeting entropy minimization. On the defensive side, the B4 TCIV module enforced a rigorous 3-layer check (Whitelist, Schema, and HMAC signature verification), and the B5 Honeypot measured worm propagation up to a maximum depth of 5 hops.
